# STARX FLOW — VISUAL REFERENCE DESIGN ANALYSIS
## Deconstruction of Attached Visual Reference (`media_1794669178766_c52da882.jpg`)

---

### 1. Spatial Canvas & Overall Composition
- **Native Dimensions**: 866 x 1024 px (Aspect Ratio: ~0.846 portrait).
- **Global Theme**: Dark obsidian & forest-green spatial intelligence environment (`#020C09` to `#05251B`).
- **Environmental Lighting**: Radiant emerald illumination (`#28DFA0`, `#087A51`), soft teal reflections, and glowing architectural edges.
- **Structural Partitioning**: Rather than flat rectangular sections, scenes are separated by sweeping organic curved glass waves that transition naturally as the user scrolls.
