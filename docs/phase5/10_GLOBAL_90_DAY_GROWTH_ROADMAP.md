## 1. STRATEGIC EXECUTION PRINCIPLES

1. **Evidence-Based Sequencing:** Technical and architectural stability must always precede promotional distribution.
2. **Focus on High-Intent Commercial Inbound:** Target service business operators who have an active, burning operational bottleneck (missed calls, double-bookings, high marketplace commission fees).
3. **No Paid Acquisition Burn:** Build durable, long-term organic discovery through traditional search engines, AI answer engines, and verified B2B software directories.
