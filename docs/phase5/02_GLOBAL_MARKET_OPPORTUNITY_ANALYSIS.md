## 1. GLOBAL MARKET EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

To transform StarX-Flow's search visibility into qualified international customer acquisition, we evaluate potential markets across 8 objective dimensions:

1. **Messaging Channel Fit:** What percentage of consumers and local service businesses conduct commerce and bookings over WhatsApp and Instagram Direct vs. SMS or email?
2. **Search Demand & Commercial Intent:** Volume of English-language search queries seeking AI business automation, AI receptionists, and booking chatbots.
3. **Willingness to Pay & Price Sensitivity:** Tolerance for SaaS subscription pricing (€29–€97/month or $29–$99/month) vs. demand for free or ad-supported tools.
4. **Regulatory & Privacy Environment:** Complexity of local compliance (GDPR/ePrivacy in the EU/UK, HIPAA in the US, DPDP in India).
5. **Technical Infrastructure Compatibility:** Local availability of supported payment gateways (Stripe), calendar providers (Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook), and cloud telephony.
6. **Language & Localization Readiness:** Viability of English-language UI and AI knowledge bases.
7. **Competitive Density:** Presence of entrenched local competitors (Mindbody, Fresha, Phorest, ManyChat).
8. **Customer Acquisition Efficiency:** Organic search competitiveness, LinkedIn professional engagement, and SaaS directory referral density.
