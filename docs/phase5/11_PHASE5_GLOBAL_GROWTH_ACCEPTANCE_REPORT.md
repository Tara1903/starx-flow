## 2. GLOBAL KEYWORD RESEARCH FINDINGS

The keyword research database covers 6 strategic clusters:
1. **AI Business Automation:** High commercial volume for `ai business automation software`, `ai automation for small businesses`, and `ai workflow automation`.
2. **WhatsApp Automation:** Urgent transactional intent for `whatsapp appointment booking automation`, `whatsapp ai chatbot for business`, and `whatsapp crm automation`.
3. **Instagram Automation:** Rapidly growing social commerce intent for `instagram dm automation software` and `instagram appointment scheduling`.
4. **AI Customer Support & Reception:** Prime high-ticket replacement intent for `ai receptionist software`, `ai virtual receptionist`, and `ai lead qualification software`.
5. **Vertical Industry Automation:** Highly targeted, low-competition queries across 13 service trades, led by dental clinics (`ai receptionist for dental clinics`), fitness studios (`whatsapp automation for gyms`), and salons (`salon appointment booking automation`).
6. **Competitor Alternatives:** Immediate high-intent evaluation queries for `manychat alternatives`, `mindbody alternatives`, `wati alternatives`, and `calendly alternatives`.

*Methodology Standard:* Data is strictly tethered to verified B2B SaaS distributions; where granular third-party API volume data is speculative, metrics are recorded as "Unavailable" rather than fabricated.
