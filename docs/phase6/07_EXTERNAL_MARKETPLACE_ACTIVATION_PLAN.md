## 3. Directory Governance & Prohibitions

1. **NO Google Business Profile (GBP):** As established in Phase 4 and Phase 5.1, StarX-Flow operates as a pure-play digital SaaS platform without a physical walk-in customer facility. Renting virtual mailboxes or publishing residential addresses violates Google Maps guidelines and is strictly prohibited.
2. **NO Fabricated Reviews:** It is strictly prohibited to purchase fake 5-star ratings or solicit reviews from non-users. All early reviews must originate from verified beta users and genuine trial accounts.
3. **NO Unsupported Capability Claims:** Listings must not claim multi-staff resource pooling within a single tenant or automated telephone voice calls until those features are completed and verified in production.
