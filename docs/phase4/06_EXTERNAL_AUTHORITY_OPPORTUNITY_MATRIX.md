## 1. AUTHORITY ETHICS & NON-MANIPULATION POLICY

Search engine algorithms (Google Spam Policies) and AI answer engines aggressively devalue or penalize domains associated with manipulative link building:
- **STRICTLY PROHIBITED:** Paid backlink schemes, private blog networks (PBNs), automated link exchanges, directory blast services, sponsored guest post farms, and syndicated fake reviews.
- **LEGITIMATE EXTERNAL AUTHORITY:** High-trust, editorial-moderated software directories, startup registries, verified partner directories, and authentic product discovery platforms. These platforms provide independent entity co-citation and genuine referral traffic from business owners looking for automation software.
