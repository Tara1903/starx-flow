## 1. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY & INTENT CLASSIFICATION

Phase 3 keyword research targets two distinct high-intent clusters:
1. **Vertical Industry Intent:** Service business owners searching for automation tailored to their exact sector (e.g., "AI receptionist for dental clinic", "WhatsApp automation for gyms").
2. **Competitor Alternative Intent:** Software buyers searching for modern, conversational, or cost-effective alternatives to established incumbent software (e.g., "Mindbody alternatives", "ManyChat alternatives for WhatsApp", "Acuity Scheduling alternatives").

### Search Intent Categories
* **Transactional / Commercial High-Intent (T/C):** High willingness to pay, immediate trial/purchase intent.
* **Investigative / Comparative Intent (I/C):** Comparing software features, seeking specific capability tradeoffs.
* **Problem-Solving Intent (P/S):** Pain-driven search (e.g., "how to stop salon no-shows on WhatsApp").
