## 2. Deep-Dive Experiment Specifications

### 2.1 Experiment 1: Founder-Led Direct Personalized Outreach
- **Target Audience:** Independent salon owners and private clinic practice managers in Manchester, Birmingham, and London with active Instagram accounts (>1.5k followers) and WhatsApp booking links in bio.
- **Hypothesis:** Reaching out directly to 30 highly qualified salon owners addressing their specific after-hours messaging pain will yield at least 6 discovery conversations and 2 pilot signups.
- **Execution Method:**
  1. Founder manually inspects public salon profiles.
  2. Sends a 3-sentence non-spam message via public business channel:
     > *"Hi [Name], loved your recent work on the caramel balayage! Quick question: how does your team manage appointment inquiries that come through your Instagram on Sunday evenings when you're closed? We built a simple front-desk AI assistant for UK salons that books appointments 24/7 directly into your calendar. Would love to send a 60-second video demo if you're curious — no worries if you're all set!"*
- **Cost:** £0.00 (Zero advertising budget).
- **Time Investment:** 1 hour per day (10 personalized messages).
- **Success Metric:** ≥ 20% reply rate; ≥ 5% pilot conversion rate.
- **Measurement Window:** 14 calendar days.
- **Stop Condition:** If 20 messages sent produce < 2 replies, pause and revise messaging hook.

---

### 2.2 Experiment 2: 60-Second Video Demonstrations ("Sunday Night Booking")
- **Target Audience:** Salon owners, aesthetic practitioners, and clinic managers on LinkedIn and Instagram.
- **Hypothesis:** A short, screen-recorded video showing a real mobile phone sending a message to a StarX-Flow assistant at 9:15 PM and receiving a confirmed booking will generate higher trust and click-through than generic text posts.
- **Execution Method:**
  1. Record a 60-second video using the StarX-Flow Web Sandbox.
  2. Side-by-side screen: Left side shows customer texting; right side shows StarX-Flow dashboard and Google Calendar automatically reserving the slot.
  3. Clear caption and CTA: *"How many bookings did your salon miss last Sunday night? Test our live sandbox in 2 minutes: starx-flow.runs-on.dev/demo"*
- **Cost:** £0.00 (Screen recording and organic posting).
- **Time Investment:** 3 hours to script, record, and edit.
- **Success Metric:** ≥ 5 inbound demo form submissions from video viewers.
- **Measurement Window:** 21 calendar days.
- **Stop Condition:** If 3 videos produce zero website demo requests, re-evaluate video angle.

---

### 2.3 Experiment 3: Local Agency & Web Designer Referral Partnerships
- **Target Audience:** Freelance web designers and small digital marketing agencies in the UK and Ireland specializing in clinic, dental, or salon websites.
- **Hypothesis:** Web designers are constantly asked by salon and clinic clients to add "chatbots" or "automated booking", but hate building custom software. Offering a simple revenue-share or client co-setup arrangement will create an ongoing referral stream.
- **Execution Method:**
  1. Identify 15 UK web agencies showcasing salon/clinic clients in their portfolios.
  2. Founder email to agency principal:
     > *"Hi [Name], notice you build fantastic websites for aesthetics clinics across the UK. Many clinic clients struggle with after-hours booking drop-offs from their websites. We built StarX-Flow — a lightweight 1-line script that adds an AI front-desk receptionist to any website. We'd love to partner with your agency to offer this to your clients with a 25% recurring rev-share."*
- **Cost:** £0.00 upfront.
- **Time Investment:** 4 hours total outreach.
- **Success Metric:** At least 2 agencies agreeing to recommend StarX-Flow to their next salon/clinic client.
- **Measurement Window:** 30 calendar days.
- **Stop Condition:** If 15 outreach emails yield zero agency responses, terminate agency channel.

## 3. Governance & Anti-Spam Safeguards

- **Strict Owner Authorization:** No outbound emails, messages, or partnership proposals will be dispatched without the platform owner's explicit written approval.
- **Zero Unsolicited Bulk Lists:** All contact is 100% individual, manual, and researched. No automated scrapers or bulk sending software.
- **Immediate Opt-Out Respect:** Anyone expressing disinterest is logged and never contacted again.
