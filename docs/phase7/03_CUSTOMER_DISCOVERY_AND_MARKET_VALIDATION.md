### Rationale for Selected Segments:
1. **Segment 1 — Hair & Beauty Salons (Rank 1 - Score 4.75):** Salon owners and stylists spend their entire working day with clients; they literally cannot pick up the phone or answer Instagram DMs while applying color or performing treatments. An automated assistant that answers pricing, verifies availability, and captures bookings solves an acute, daily operational headache with near-zero regulatory friction.
2. **Segment 2 — Dental & Private Aesthetic Clinics (Rank 2 - Score 4.55):** High average customer transaction value (£80 to £500+ per appointment). A single new patient captured during evening hours recovers months of software cost. Practice managers are eager to reduce front-desk telephone load.

## 2. Customer Discovery Interview Framework

### 2.1 Discovery Protocol (20-Minute Structured Conversation)
The objective of discovery is not to pitch software, but to rigorously understand how the business handles customer communications today, where their revenue leaks, and what tools they already pay for.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SECTION 1: Operational Workflow Baseline (5 mins)                           |
| - How do new clients find and contact you? (Phone, WhatsApp, IG, Website)    |
| - Who currently handles messages, and when?                                 |
| - What happens to inquiries that arrive between 6 PM and 9 AM?              |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                      |
                                      v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SECTION 2: Pain Point & Leakage Identification (5 mins)                     |
| - What are the top 3 most common questions people ask before booking?       |
| - How often do people ask for slots, get slow responses, and disappear?     |
| - How many hours per day does staff spend typing the same answers?          |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                      |
                                      v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SECTION 3: Current Tool Stack & Frustrations (5 mins)                       |
| - What booking software or calendar do you currently use? (Fresha, Timely)  |
| - Why haven't online booking links solved this completely?                   |
| - Have you tried chatbots before? What failed?                              |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                      |
                                      v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SECTION 4: Commercial Economics & Solution Validation (5 mins)              |
| - If an AI handled 80% of routine questions and booked appointments,        |
|   what would that be worth to your practice each month?                     |
| - Would you be open to trialing this for 14 days on a pilot basis?          |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 3. Customer Discovery Questionnaire & Script

### Opening & Warm-Up
> *"Hi [Name], thank you so much for taking 15 minutes to speak with me. We are researching how independent [salons / private clinics] in the [UK / Ireland] manage incoming customer messages and booking requests outside normal working hours. I am not here to sell you anything today — my only goal is to understand your day-to-day front-desk workflow."*

### Key Diagnostic Questions:
1. **Volume & Channels:** *"On an average day, how many inquiries come in through WhatsApp, Instagram, or your website versus regular phone calls?"*
2. **After-Hours Behavior:** *"What happens when someone messages your salon at 9:30 PM on a Sunday asking for an urgent appointment on Tuesday?"*
3. **Repetitive Question Burden:** *"If you could eliminate answering three questions over and over again, which three would they be?"*
4. **Current Tech Stack:** *"Which booking software or calendar does your team rely on right now? (e.g., Fresha, Phorest, Treatwell, Cliniko, Google Calendar)?"*
5. **The Booking Drop-off Problem:** *"Do you ever notice clients start asking questions on chat, but when you tell them 'Go to our website booking link', they never actually complete the booking?"*
6. **Economics of Missed Leads:** *"What is an average new client worth to your business in year one? If you lose 3 after-hours inquiries a week, how much revenue is that per month?"*
7. **Human Control & Trust:** *"If an AI assistant was answering questions on your behalf, what is the single biggest fear or risk you would want to prevent?"*

## 4. Customer Pain-Point Taxonomy

Through initial customer workflow modeling, customer friction points are classified into 4 distinct quadrants:

```
                  HIGH SEVERITY
                       |
     [A. Revenue Leakage]   |   [B. Staff Burnout]
  - After-hours booking drop | - Constant phone interruptions
  - Weekend inquiry silence  | - Repetitive FAQ typing
  - No deposit / no-show risk| - High front-desk turnover
                       |
  ---------------------+--------------------- FREQUENCY
                       |
     [C. Tool Disconnect]    |   [D. Trust & Risk]
  - Booking links ignored    | - Fear of AI hallucinations
  - Double booking fears     | - Loss of personal touch
  - Multiple disjointed apps | - Customer privacy concerns
                       |
                  LOW SEVERITY
```

## 5. Feedback Capture & Product Synthesis Process

All discovery findings must be recorded in an internal structured feedback log capturing:
- **Prospect ID & Business Name**
- **Decision-Maker Title** (Owner, Practice Manager, Receptionist)
- **Primary Inquiry Channel** (WhatsApp vs. Instagram vs. Web)
- **Existing Calendar / Booking Software**
- **Estimated Monthly Missed Inquiries**
- **Top 3 Requested FAQs**
- **Pilot Readiness** (Ready to test / Needs more trust / Disqualified)
- **Verbatim Quotes** (to refine positioning copy without fabrication)
