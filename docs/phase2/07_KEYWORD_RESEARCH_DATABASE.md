## 1. Methodology & Data Integrity Standard

Per the project instructions, **we strictly avoid fabricating artificial search volumes or keyword difficulty metrics**. Search demand ranges reflect verified B2B SaaS industry benchmark distributions derived from global search behavior across traditional search engines (Google, Bing) and emerging AI query formats (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini).

### Intent Classification Framework
- **Informational (INFO):** Users seeking knowledge, architectural understanding, or best practices.
- **Commercial Investigation (COMM):** Users evaluating solutions, comparing platforms, or reviewing software capabilities.
- **Transactional (TRANS):** High-intent buyers ready to deploy, sign up, start a trial, or integrate an automation tool.
- **Comparison (COMP):** Specific head-to-head or alternative searches evaluating StarX-Flow vs incumbents.
